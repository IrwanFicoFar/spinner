# Crowd applause

`crowd-applause.mp3` is an 8.5 second excerpt (starting at 4 seconds), normalized, faded, and converted to MP3 from **Sound Effects - Applause after a concert** by **Amada44**.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sound_Effects_-_Applause_after_a_concert.ogg
- Original: https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/3/32/Sound_Effects_-_Applause_after_a_concert.ogg
- License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Retrieved: 2026-10-16

The asset is served locally. Playback makes no requests to the source website. Other percussion and fanfare effects are synthesized with Web Audio.
